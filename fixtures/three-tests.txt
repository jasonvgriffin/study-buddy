Practice Test 1

Section: Rivers

1. Which river runs through Cairo?
a) Nile
b) Amazon
c) Danube
d) Rhine

2. Which two of these rivers are in Europe? (Choose two)
a) Danube
b) Nile
c) Rhine
d) Amazon

Section: Capitals

3. What is the capital of Portugal?
a) Lisbon
b) Madrid
c) Porto

Answer Key
1. a
2. a and c
3. a

Detailed Answers
1. a) Nile
The Answer: a) Nile
Cairo sits on the Nile in Egypt.
The incorrect answers:
b) Amazon
The Amazon is in South America.
c) Danube
The Danube is in Europe but does not run through Cairo.
d) Rhine
The Rhine does not run through Cairo.

2. a) Danube and c) Rhine
The Answer: a and c
The Danube and the Rhine both flow through Europe.
The incorrect answers:
b) Nile
The Nile is in Africa.
d) Amazon
The Amazon is in South America.

3. a) Lisbon

Practice Test 2

Section: Weather

1. What does a barometer measure?
a) Wind speed
b) Air pressure
c) Rainfall
d) Humidity

2. Which cloud is tall and stormy?
a) Cirrus
b) Cumulonimbus
c) Stratus

Answer Key
1. b
2. b

Detailed Answers
1. b) Air pressure
The Answer: b) Air pressure
A barometer measures the pressure of the air.
The incorrect answers:
a) Wind speed
Wind speed is measured with an anemometer.
c) Rainfall
Rainfall is measured with a rain gauge.
d) Humidity
Humidity is measured with a hygrometer.

2. b) Cumulonimbus
The Answer: b) Cumulonimbus
Cumulonimbus clouds tower upward and bring storms.
The incorrect answers:
a) Cirrus
Cirrus clouds are thin and high.
c) Stratus
Stratus clouds form a flat gray layer.
